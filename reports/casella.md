## Notes from the agreement

Consolidated EBITDA starts from Consolidated Adjusted Net Income, which adds back stock compensation, impairments (up to $50M cumulative), severance and reorganization costs (up to $10M) and acquisition costs, then adds interest, taxes, depreciation, depletion and amortization. Acquisitions count pro forma, with synergies up to 15% of EBITDA. Only unencumbered cash up to $100M is netted against debt. Interest coverage uses cash interest charges, not total interest expense. The agreement writes the leverage limit as '4:00 to 1.00', which reads as 4.00x.
