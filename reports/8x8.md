## Notes from the agreement

All three covenants use Adjusted Cash EBITDA, which adds back D&A (including capitalized software and deferred commission amortization), impairments, stock compensation and unusual charges (capped at 15%), but subtracts cash spent on capitalized software and deferred sales commissions in the period. Public data can't see those cash deductions, so the tool's EBITDA runs high for 8x8. Interest coverage uses interest paid or payable in cash. Netted cash is capped at 50% of Adjusted Cash EBITDA. The First Amendment (2025) removed the scheduled step-downs to 4.25x and 4.00x.
